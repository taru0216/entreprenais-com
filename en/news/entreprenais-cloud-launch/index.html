EntreprenAIs Cloud, a Green AI Cloud, Now Available

Taruishi Digital Technology Research Institute LLC (TDRI) has today launched EntreprenAIs Cloud, a green AI cloud designed to combine ultra-high speed with ultra-low power consumption. It runs on our own green data center, built around transparency in power sourcing.

A hybrid architecture: Cerebral AI × Agent Spinal Reflex technology

EntreprenAIs Cloud is modeled loosely on the human nervous system, with two layers working together. A "Cerebral AI" — a local LLM running inside our own green data center — handles complex reasoning and generation that requires slow, careful thought. An "Agent Spinal Reflex" technology handles routine responses instantly on CPU, with no LLM inference involved. The two layers switch seamlessly within a single conversation; when a reflex response isn't enough, the exchange is handed off to the Cerebral AI without breaking the flow.

This handoff is powered by our proprietary compilation technology, which converts a design (SKILL.md) written by the Cerebral AI into a lightweight executable (skill.py) that runs on CPU alone — one of the two pillars behind our speed. This process is currently semi-automatic, running alongside human oversight, and we are progressively expanding the range it can handle.

Transparent power sourcing through our own green data center

EntreprenAIs Cloud runs on a green data center powered by our own solar generation and renewable energy contracts. By removing round-trip calls to external cloud LLMs and dependence on other companies' data center power mixes, we aim to keep the power sourcing fully in-house and support transparency backed by disclosure and audit trails.

Renewable power comes with real constraints on output and supply. Our hybrid architecture grew directly out of the need to minimize power consumption within those constraints — the combination of "ultra-high speed" and "ultra-low power" is a consequence of designing around that limit, not a separate feature bolted on afterward.

A note on figures and claims: Performance claims such as "ultra-high speed," "ultra-low power," and response-time figures for the Agent Spinal Reflex technology are based on our own internal measurements under our own testing conditions, and do not guarantee performance across all requests or environments. References to "effectively 100% renewable" power sourcing are based on our own solar generation and renewable energy contracts. We are continuing to improve the transparency and disclosure detail of our measurement conditions. See also the Architecture and Green sections on our homepage.
