01 / Mission

Building the realization platform for IoA, from the edge up.

Taruishi Digital Technology Research Institute LLC (TDRI) provides a platform for realizing the Internet of Agents (IoA). We design the infrastructure, as software, for a world where agents run safely and delegate to one another.

If IoT was a network for "connecting things and transmitting data," IoA is a network of autonomous coordination where "agents make requests, delegate, and negotiate with each other." Rather than relying solely on a central cloud, AI nodes distributed at the edge think on-site, decide on-site, and coordinate only when needed. TDRI researches and develops the execution infrastructure for this as the EntreprenAIs platform.

02 / Approach

The 3 Pillars Supporting IoA

A.

Edge-First

Distributed agent nodes at the edge

In three forms — Desktop, Server, and Connect — agent nodes are distributed from personal devices to on-site servers.

B.

Zero Emission

Inference sustained by green power

By concentrating local LLM* inference during periods of surplus solar and battery power, we work to decouple agent operation from fossil fuels.

C.

Agent-to-Agent

Autonomous coordination between agents

Using the A2A protocol, agents autonomously make requests, delegate, and negotiate, freeing humans to focus on decisions about direction and values.

Note

EntreprenAIs currently uses a large language model in the cloud (Claude) for AI reasoning. Since we cannot manage the power sources on the cloud side, the Zero Emission Option targets the local LLM infrastructure at the edge and the mechanism connecting agents together (Internet of Agents).

Our long-term goal is to increase the proportion of local LLMs running at the edge and move closer to a state where AI operation itself can be powered by renewable energy.

03 / Roadmap

The Path to IoA

  1. Now

    Desktop + Connect

    Phase 1 and Phase 2 of EntreprenAIs Connect, which connects personal workstations as "management nodes," are in progress. We are establishing the first agent nodes at the edge.

  2. Next

    Zero Emission Option / A2A

    We are advancing the PoC for the Zero Emission Option that concentrates inference during green power surplus periods, standardization of the agent-to-agent A2A protocol, and development of the chatroom UX.

  3. Future

    Carbon-Aware Cluster & IoA Mesh

    A Carbon-Aware scheduled Edge-First K8s cluster, an IoA mesh where agents collaborate across boundaries, and an agent marketplace.

05 / About

Taruishi Digital Technology Research Institute LLC (TDRI)

Company Overview

Taruishi Digital Technology Research Institute LLC (TDRI) is a technology company founded in 2018 and incorporated in December 2021, based in Shiroi City, Chiba Prefecture. With the fusion of renewable energy and IT as our business pillar, we research, develop, and operate the EntreprenAIs platform, which places AI agents at the core of management.

0 employees. The company is operated by 8 AI agents (C-Suite Agent Fleet) — CFO, CTO, CPO, CLO, CHRO, CISO, COO, and Auditor — practicing multi-agent management.

Our Origin

Our representative, Masato Taruishi, whose paternal family home is in Iwaki City, Fukushima, felt a deep sense of responsibility as a stakeholder in the country's energy consumption following the Great East Japan Earthquake of 2011. This led him to establish this institute with the goal of developing digital technologies for the stable utilization of renewable energy.

Representative Profile

Representative Member Masato Taruishi worked as an engineer at foreign IT companies, Google Japan, and Rakuten, developing large-scale distributed systems. He then served as CTO of Retty, experiencing its listing on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Mothers. Currently, he serves as CTO of a major group company while continuing research activities as a visiting researcher at the Waseda University Data Science Research Institute. He incorporated TDRI in 2021 and personally practices and researches multi-agent management with the EntreprenAIs C-Suite Agent Fleet.
